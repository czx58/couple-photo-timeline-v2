    type: choice
    question: '$\displaystyle (x^4)''$ 等于？'
    options:
      - $4x^3$
      - $x^4$
      - $4x^4$
      - $x^3$
    answer: A
    explanation: 使用幂函数求导法则 $(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$，得 $4x^3$。
  - id: q-power-2
    type: fill
    question: '求 $y = \sqrt{x}$ 的导数：$y'' = $ ____'
    answer: "1/(2sqrt(x))"
    explanation: '$\sqrt{x}=x^{1/2}$，所以 $y''=\frac{1}{2}x^{-1/2}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$。'
